## Short description

QuantumPools is a multi-chain liquidity-pool management and analytics application for concentrated-liquidity position bookkeeping, fee tracking, impermanent loss, and deposit-versus-hold performance analysis.

## Additional product information

The project covers EVM and Solana liquidity positions, with venue coverage including Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Aerodrome, Raydium, Orca, and Meteora. Indexing, analytics, manual-entry support, and liquidity-management features vary by venue and network. Snuggle rebalancing is a liquidity-management feature where supported; it is not a guarantee of returns.
