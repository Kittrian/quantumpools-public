## Short description

QuantumPools is a multi-chain concentrated-liquidity manager and analytics platform. It covers Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Aerodrome, Orca, Meteora, and Solana venues (including Base), with per-position LP bookkeeping, Cover Value–style deposit-versus-hold reporting, multi-sleeve range strategies, owner-tunable rebalance timing, and ETH hedging workflows. On supported vault deployments, LP NFTs can sit in a QuantumPools vault with always-available withdraw, timelocked owner controls, and a hard-capped performance fee on trading fees only — never on principal.

## Additional product information

Venue coverage includes Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Aerodrome, Raydium, Orca, and Meteora across EVM networks (including Base) and Solana.
